Privacy Policy for Weather Activity Planner

Last updated: 26 July 2026

Weather Activity Planner ("the app") does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Data Collection

The app does not require an account, sign-in, or any personal information to function. It does not access your device's location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage. City search is performed by typing a city name directly - the app never reads your device's GPS or network-based location.

Third-Party Services

The app sends the city name you search for, and the coordinates of the city you select, to Open-Meteo's public weather and geocoding APIs, over HTTPS, in order to display weather forecasts. No account identifiers, device identifiers, or other personal data are sent with these requests. See Open-Meteo's terms for details on how they handle API requests.

Data Storage

The app temporarily caches recent forecast results in memory on your device to allow the app to keep working briefly if your connection drops. This cache is never transmitted anywhere and is cleared when the app is closed.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to pnow.dev@gmail.com.

Weather Activity Planner is an independent project and is not affiliated with Open-Meteo.
