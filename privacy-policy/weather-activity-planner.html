Privacy Policy for Weather Activity Planner

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Weather Activity Planner ("the app") does not require an account and does not collect, store, or share any personal data on any server controlled by us.

Location Data

With your permission, the app can use your device's precise or approximate location (via Android's location services) to show weather for your current position. Location access is optional: you can decline the permission and instead search for a city by name. If you grant permission, your coordinates are used only to request a forecast and are not stored on the device beyond the in-memory cache described below, and are never linked to your identity.

Data Collection

The app does not require sign-in and does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or device storage.

Third-Party Services

To display weather forecasts, the app sends either the city name you search for, or your device coordinates (if you chose to share your location), to Open-Meteo's public weather and geocoding APIs over HTTPS. No account identifiers or other personal data are sent with these requests. See Open-Meteo's terms for details on how they handle API requests.

Data Storage

The app temporarily caches recent forecast results in memory on your device to allow the app to keep working briefly if your connection drops. This cache is never transmitted anywhere and is cleared when the app is closed.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to pnow.dev@gmail.com.

Weather Activity Planner is an independent project and is not affiliated with Open-Meteo.
