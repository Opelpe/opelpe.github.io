Privacy Policy for Weather Activity Planner

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Weather Activity Planner ("the app") does not require an account and does not collect, store, or share any personal data on any server controlled by us.

Location Data

With your permission, the app can use your device's precise or approximate location (via Android's location services) to show weather for your current position. Location access is optional: you can decline the permission and instead search for a city by name. If you grant permission, your coordinates are sent to LocationIQ to resolve them into a place name (see below), used to request a forecast, and are not stored on the device beyond the in-memory cache described below. Your coordinates are never linked to your identity.

Data Collection

The app does not require sign-in and does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or device storage.

Third-Party Services

The app uses two third-party APIs over HTTPS, neither of which receives any account identifiers or other personal data:

  • Open-Meteo - receives the coordinates of your selected or current location to return a weather forecast. See Open-Meteo's terms.
  • LocationIQ - receives the city name you type when searching, or your device's coordinates when you use "current location", to look up matching places or resolve coordinates into a place name. Requests are authenticated with an app-level API key, not anything tied to you personally. See LocationIQ's privacy policy.

Data Storage

The app temporarily caches recent forecast results in memory on your device to allow the app to keep working briefly if your connection drops. This cache is never transmitted anywhere and is cleared when the app is closed.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to pnow.dev@gmail.com.

Weather Activity Planner is an independent project and is not affiliated with Open-Meteo or LocationIQ.
